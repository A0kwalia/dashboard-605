Predicted risk, individual crashes

Every reported crash in the 2024 test year, positioned by coordinates and coloured by the risk the model predicted for it. Filter by predicted risk, actual outcome and lighting; hover any point for its conditions and what actually happened.


     

Compare the two outcome filters. Casualty and non-casualty crashes occupy much the same space and much the same colour range, which is the spatial expression of ROC-AUC 0.634 — real separation, but modest. Note also that high-risk points do not cluster geographically: the signal comes from road and environmental conditions, not location.
