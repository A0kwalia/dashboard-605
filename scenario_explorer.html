What drives this estimate

Change in KSI probability caused by each factor, compared with the conditions of a typical reported crash. Factors you did not change are held at the most common value and are marked typical. These are associations in reported crashes, not causes: wet roads, for example, produce many more minor crashes, so a smaller share of wet-road crashes are KSI.
