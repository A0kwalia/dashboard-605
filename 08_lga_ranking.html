Highest predicted-risk LGAs

The fifteen LGAs with the highest mean predicted KSI risk, among those with at least 150 crashes in 2024. Hover for the observed KSI rate and the prediction gap.

Port Macquarie-Hastings ranks first at 27.8%. 14 of these 15 LGAs had a higher observed KSI rate than predicted, by up to 12.1 points (Cessnock), consistent with 2024's higher statewide KSI rate. Use the order, not the exact values. Areas marked "low model reliability" had a 2024 ROC-AUC below 0.60.
