Risk map by local government area

Mean predicted KSI risk per LGA across the 2024 test year, for LGAs with at least 60 crashes. Bubble size is crash volume, colour is predicted risk. Drag the slider to highlight LGAs above a chosen threshold.

Mean predicted KSI risk ranges from 7.7% (Queanbeyan-Palerang Regional) to 28.4% (Wollondilly). Inland and regional LGAs (west of 150.5°E) average 21.5% against 22.8% for coastal and metropolitan LGAs. The map summarises the model's output by area; it does not mean location itself causes the risk.

Source: KSI model (histogram gradient boosting, sigmoid calibration), trained on 2020–2023 and evaluated on the held-out 2024 year · NSW Road Crash Data 2020–2024, Transport for NSW (CC BY 4.0) · Hover any element for exact values.
