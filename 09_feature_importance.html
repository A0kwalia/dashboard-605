What the model relies on

Drop in ROC-AUC when each feature is randomly shuffled, measured on a held-out 2024 sample so it reflects generalisation rather than training fit. The twelve most important of 24 features are shown.

Primary permanent feature and speed limit carry the most signal. Unlike the earlier casualty model, location now matters: LGA, latitude, longitude and conurbation together account for an ROC-AUC drop of 0.028. Bar length shows what the model uses, not how much risk a condition causes; the Scenario explorer shows the direction of each factor for a specific site.
